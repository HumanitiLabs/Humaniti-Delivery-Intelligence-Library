# Decision Latency Index™ (DLI)

Version 1.0

The Decision Latency Index™ measures how long critical delivery decisions take to move from identification to execution.

---

# Why It Matters

Most ERP firms measure:

* project status
* budgets
* utilization

Few measure:

* decision speed

Yet delayed decisions create:

* project delays
* delivery bottlenecks
* resource conflicts
* escalation events
* margin erosion

Decision Latency is often the hidden constraint limiting delivery capacity.

---

# The Decision Flow

Issue Identified

↓

Decision Requested

↓

Decision Assigned

↓

Decision Approved

↓

Decision Executed

---

# Measurement

For each decision:

Measure:

1. Time to Request
2. Time to Assign
3. Time to Approve
4. Time to Execute

Total Time = Decision Latency

---

# DLI Scoring

## Elite

0-24 Hours

Delivery organizations operate with minimal friction.

---

## Healthy

1-3 Days

Minor delays exist.

---

## Moderate Risk

4-7 Days

Operational friction becoming visible.

---

## High Risk

8-14 Days

Delivery capacity negatively impacted.

---

## Critical Risk

15+ Days

Governance and delivery breakdown likely.

---

# Root Causes of High DLI

Unclear ownership

Approval bottlenecks

Governance gaps

Stakeholder confusion

Escalation dependency

Executive overload

---

# Recommended Actions

If DLI exceeds 7 days:

* Conduct Delivery Governance Audit™
* Review Escalation Paths
* Review Approval Matrix
* Review Resource Ownership
