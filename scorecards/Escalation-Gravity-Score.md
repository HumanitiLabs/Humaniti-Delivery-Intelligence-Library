# Escalation Gravity Score™ (EGS)

Version 1.0

The Escalation Gravity Score™ measures how strongly delivery issues are pulled upward through the organization.

Most ERP Implementation Firms and Systems Integrators measure project status.

Few measure escalation dependency.

Organizations with high escalation dependency often experience:

* delivery bottlenecks
* overloaded leadership
* slow decision making
* reduced delivery capacity
* operational fragility

The Escalation Gravity Score™ exists to measure the hidden organizational force that pulls work upward instead of resolving it at the appropriate level.

---

# Core Principle

Healthy organizations resolve issues at the lowest responsible level.

Unhealthy organizations escalate everything upward.

The higher the escalation pull, the weaker the delivery operating system.

# Escalation Gravity Formula

Level 1 Escalation = 1 Point

Level 2 Escalation = 2 Points

Level 3 Escalation = 3 Points

Level 4 Escalation = 4 Points

Level 5 Escalation = 5 Points

Level 6 Escalation = 6 Points

EGS = Total Escalation Points ÷ Total Escalations

# Escalation Gravity Levels

## Level 1 — Healthy Delivery System

EGS < 2

Most issues resolved locally.

---

## Level 2 — Growing Friction

EGS 2–3

Delivery managers increasingly involved.

---

## Level 3 — Governance Stress

EGS 3–4

Directors frequently involved.

---

## Level 4 — Executive Dependency

EGS 4–5

Executives acting as operational bottlenecks.

---

## Level 5 — Organizational Fragility

EGS > 5

Leadership has become the delivery operating system.
