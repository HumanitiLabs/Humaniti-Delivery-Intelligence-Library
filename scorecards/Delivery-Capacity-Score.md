# Delivery Capacity Score™ (DCS)

Version 1.0

The Delivery Capacity Score™ (DCS) measures an organization's ability to absorb additional delivery demand without creating operational instability, delivery delays, governance friction, resource overload, or margin erosion.

Most ERP Implementation Firms and Systems Integrators assume capacity is determined by headcount.

In reality, capacity is determined by the organization's ability to scale resources, operations, governance, knowledge, and leadership simultaneously.

The Delivery Capacity Score™ provides a measurable indicator of an organization's true delivery scalability.

---

# Why It Matters

Organizations rarely fail because demand exceeds capacity.

Organizations fail because they incorrectly estimate capacity.

Common symptoms include:

* overloaded consultants
* delayed project delivery
* executive intervention
* hiring emergencies
* declining margins
* client dissatisfaction

The Delivery Capacity Score™ identifies these constraints before they become delivery failures.

---

# Score Components

The Delivery Capacity Score™ is calculated using five dimensions.

Each dimension contributes 20 points.

Maximum Score:

100

---

# Component 1 — Resource Capacity

Measures:

* staffing adequacy
* resource availability
* capacity forecasting
* allocation effectiveness

Indicators:

* predictable staffing
* low resource conflict
* strong delivery coverage

---

# Component 2 — Operational Capacity

Measures:

* workflow scalability
* process efficiency
* delivery throughput
* operational stability

Indicators:

* repeatable delivery processes
* low operational friction
* scalable execution

---

# Component 3 — Governance Capacity

Measures:

* decision speed
* approval efficiency
* escalation effectiveness
* governance scalability

Indicators:

* low decision latency
* structured governance
* minimal approval bottlenecks

---

# Component 4 — Knowledge Capacity

Measures:

* documentation quality
* onboarding effectiveness
* knowledge accessibility
* dependency reduction

Indicators:

* low key-person dependency
* strong SOP adoption
* scalable expertise

---

# Component 5 — Leadership Capacity

Measures:

* executive dependency
* leadership workload
* strategic focus
* delegation effectiveness

Indicators:

* leadership focused on growth
* limited operational intervention
* scalable management structure

# Delivery Capacity Levels

## Level 5 — Delivery Intelligence Leader

81–100

The organization can scale delivery predictably.

Growth does not create operational instability.

Capacity constraints are proactively managed.

---

## Level 4 — Scalable Delivery

61–80

Strong delivery foundation.

Minor constraints exist but do not significantly impact growth.

---

## Level 3 — Capacity Aware

41–60

Capacity limitations are visible.

Growth increasingly exposes delivery constraints.

---

## Level 2 — Capacity Friction

21–40

Delivery performance is regularly impacted by capacity limitations.

Operational stress is increasing.

---

## Level 1 — Capacity Constraint

0–20

Capacity has become a primary growth bottleneck.

Operational fragility is high.

Leadership intervention is frequent.
