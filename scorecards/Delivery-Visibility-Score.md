# Delivery Visibility Score™ (DVS)

Version 1.0

The Delivery Visibility Score™ measures an organization's ability to identify, monitor, communicate, and act on delivery intelligence before issues impact project performance.

Most organizations rely on reporting.

Delivery Intelligence™ relies on visibility.

The Delivery Visibility Score™ provides a measurable benchmark for visibility maturity.

---

# Why It Matters

Organizations rarely fail because risks appear suddenly.

Organizations fail because risks remain invisible.

Common symptoms:

* surprise escalations
* delayed decisions
* missed milestones
* inaccurate reporting
* leadership firefighting

The Delivery Visibility Score™ identifies these weaknesses before they create delivery instability.
