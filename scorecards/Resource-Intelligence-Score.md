# Resource Intelligence Score™ (RIS)

Version 1.0

The Resource Intelligence Score™ (RIS) measures an organization's ability to deploy, optimize, forecast, and govern resources to maximize delivery capacity and operational performance.

Most ERP Implementation Firms and Systems Integrators measure utilization.

Few measure resource intelligence.

The Resource Intelligence Score™ exists to provide a holistic view of resource effectiveness beyond utilization metrics.

---

# Why It Matters

Organizations do not scale because they hire more people.

Organizations scale because they deploy resources intelligently.

Poor resource intelligence creates:

* overloaded consultants
* underutilized specialists
* resource conflicts
* missed project deadlines
* key-person dependency
* forecasting failures
* margin erosion

Resource Intelligence™ transforms resource management into a strategic capability.

---

# Score Components

The Resource Intelligence Score™ is calculated using five dimensions.

Each dimension contributes 20 points.

Maximum Score:

100

---

# Component 1 — Capacity Intelligence

Measures:

* available delivery capacity
* forecast capacity
* capacity planning maturity
* resource availability visibility

Indicators:

* proactive staffing
* predictable capacity planning
* low capacity bottlenecks

---

# Component 2 — Utilization Intelligence

Measures:

* utilization consistency
* utilization sustainability
* workload balance
* billable efficiency

Indicators:

* healthy utilization levels
* minimal consultant burnout
* balanced resource deployment

---

# Component 3 — Demand Intelligence

Measures:

* delivery demand forecasting
* staffing prediction accuracy
* future resource visibility
* pipeline-to-capacity alignment

Indicators:

* predictable hiring
* low staffing surprises
* strong demand forecasting

---

# Component 4 — Dependency Intelligence

Measures:

* key-person dependency
* knowledge concentration risk
* skill concentration risk
* resource redundancy

Indicators:

* documented knowledge
* distributed expertise
* reduced delivery fragility

---

# Component 5 — Strategic Resource Intelligence

Measures:

* alignment of resources to strategic priorities
* allocation effectiveness
* delivery leverage
* resource ROI

Indicators:

* high-value projects receive top talent
* strategic initiatives properly staffed
* resource allocation supports growth objectives

# Resource Intelligence Levels

## Level 5 — Delivery Intelligence Leader

81–100

Resource allocation is optimized.

Forecasting is predictable.

Capacity scales without operational chaos.

---

## Level 4 — Resource Mature

61–80

Strong resource management with minor optimization opportunities.

---

## Level 3 — Resource Aware

41–60

Resource issues visible but not consistently managed.

---

## Level 2 — Resource Friction

21–40

Resource allocation challenges impacting delivery performance.

---

## Level 1 — Critical Resource Risk

0–20

Resource management has become a primary operational constraint.

Delivery scalability is severely limited.
