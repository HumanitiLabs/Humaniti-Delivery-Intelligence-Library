# Margin Leakage Index™ (MLI)

Version 1.0

The Margin Leakage Index™ measures the degree to which operational inefficiencies, delivery friction, governance breakdowns, and resource constraints are reducing project profitability.

Most organizations see declining margins.

Few understand why.

The Margin Leakage Index™ identifies the leading indicators of profitability erosion before margins collapse.

---

# Why It Matters

Revenue can grow while profitability declines.

The objective is not revenue growth.

The objective is profitable delivery.

---

# Index Components

## Scope Leakage

Measures:

* scope creep
* uncontrolled change requests
* project expansion

---

## Resource Leakage

Measures:

* inefficient staffing
* utilization instability
* rework costs

---

## Delivery Leakage

Measures:

* delays
* missed milestones
* schedule overruns

---

## Governance Leakage

Measures:

* decision latency
* escalation dependency
* approval bottlenecks

---

## Financial Leakage

Measures:

* forecasting inaccuracy
* margin visibility gaps
* profitability surprises
