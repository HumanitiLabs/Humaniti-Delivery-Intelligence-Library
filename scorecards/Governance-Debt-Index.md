# Governance Debt Index™ (GDI)

Version 1.0

The Governance Debt Index™ measures the accumulation of governance deficiencies that create operational friction, decision delays, escalation dependency, and delivery risk.

Organizations routinely measure:

* financial performance
* project status
* resource utilization

Few organizations measure governance debt.

Yet governance debt is often the hidden constraint limiting delivery capacity, scalability, and operational performance.

---

# Definition

Governance Debt™ is the accumulation of unresolved governance issues that increase operational friction and reduce delivery effectiveness.

Examples include:

* unclear ownership
* undocumented decision rights
* approval bottlenecks
* escalation dependency
* executive involvement in operational decisions
* inconsistent governance practices

Like technical debt, governance debt compounds over time.

---

# Governance Debt Dimensions

The Governance Debt Index™ measures five dimensions.

Each dimension is scored from 0–20.

Total Possible Score:

100

---

# Dimension 1 — Ownership Debt

Indicators:

* unclear accountability
* overlapping responsibilities
* ownership disputes
* missing RACI structures

Questions:

1. Ownership is clearly documented.
2. Accountability is consistently enforced.
3. Responsibility conflicts are rare.
4. Teams understand decision ownership.
5. Deliverables have accountable owners.

---

# Dimension 2 — Decision Debt

Indicators:

* slow decisions
* unclear decision rights
* approval confusion
* delayed execution

Questions:

1. Decision authority is documented.
2. Decisions are made quickly.
3. Approval paths are clear.
4. Decision delays rarely impact delivery.
5. Teams know who can approve decisions.

---

# Dimension 3 — Escalation Debt

Indicators:

* excessive escalations
* leadership dependency
* escalation overload
* repeated issue escalation

Questions:

1. Escalation criteria are documented.
2. Most issues are resolved locally.
3. Escalations are infrequent.
4. Escalations are resolved efficiently.
5. Escalation ownership is clear.

---

# Dimension 4 — Approval Debt

Indicators:

* excessive approvals
* approval delays
* approval duplication
* governance bottlenecks

Questions:

1. Approval processes are efficient.
2. Approval timelines are predictable.
3. Approval authority is documented.
4. Approval bottlenecks are rare.
5. Unnecessary approvals have been eliminated.

---

# Dimension 5 — Executive Dependency Debt

Indicators:

* executive involvement in routine decisions
* operational escalations reaching leadership
* leadership acting as delivery managers

Questions:

1. Executives focus on strategic decisions.
2. Operational issues rarely require executive intervention.
3. Teams are empowered to act.
4. Delegation is effective.
5. Leadership is not a delivery bottleneck.
