# Delivery Capacity Framework™

Version 1.0

The Delivery Capacity Framework™ is designed to help ERP Implementation Firms and Systems Integrators understand, measure, forecast, and increase their ability to deliver projects without increasing operational chaos.

Most firms measure utilization.

Most firms measure project volume.

Few firms understand their true delivery capacity.

Delivery Capacity™ is not the number of people in the organization.

Delivery Capacity™ is the organization's ability to consistently deliver client outcomes while maintaining profitability, quality, governance, and operational stability.

# The Five Layers of Delivery Capacity™

## Layer 1 — Resource Capacity

Can the organization deploy sufficient resources to support delivery demand?

Questions:

* How much available capacity exists?
* Which teams are overloaded?
* Which teams are underutilized?
* Where are capacity constraints forming?

Measures:

* Available Capacity
* Allocated Capacity
* Forecast Capacity
* Capacity Utilization

---

## Layer 2 — Operational Capacity

Can delivery systems support increased project volume?

Questions:

* Are delivery processes standardized?
* Are handoffs efficient?
* Are workflows repeatable?
* Can operations scale without leadership intervention?

Measures:

* Workflow Efficiency
* Process Stability
* Delivery Throughput
* Operational Friction

---

## Layer 3 — Governance Capacity

Can decisions move fast enough to support delivery growth?

Questions:

* Are approval bottlenecks limiting delivery?
* Is governance creating delays?
* Can decisions scale with project volume?

Measures:

* Decision Latency
* Escalation Volume
* Governance Debt
* Approval Cycle Time

---

## Layer 4 — Knowledge Capacity

Can delivery knowledge scale across teams?

Questions:

* Is critical knowledge documented?
* Can new team members become productive quickly?
* Does delivery depend on specific individuals?

Measures:

* Knowledge Availability
* Documentation Coverage
* Onboarding Efficiency
* Key Person Dependency

---

## Layer 5 — Leadership Capacity

Can leadership support growth without becoming the operating system?

Questions:

* Are executives involved in routine decisions?
* Can delivery operate independently?
* Is leadership focused on strategy?

Measures:

* Executive Dependency
* Escalation Gravity
* Strategic Allocation
* Leadership Utilization

---

# Delivery Capacity Formula™

Delivery Capacity is constrained by the weakest layer.

Increasing headcount does not automatically increase capacity.

Capacity increases when:

* resources scale
* operations scale
* governance scales
* knowledge scales
* leadership scales

together.
