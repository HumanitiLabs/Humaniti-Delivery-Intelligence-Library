# Delivery Governance Framework™

Version 1.0

The Delivery Governance Framework™ is designed to help ERP Implementation Firms and Systems Integrators create scalable decision-making, accountability, ownership, and escalation systems.

Most delivery organizations do not fail because of poor project management.

They fail because decisions move too slowly, ownership is unclear, governance is inconsistent, and leadership becomes the default escalation path.

Delivery Governance™ exists to create operational clarity before delivery friction becomes delivery failure.

# The Five Layers of Delivery Governance™

## Layer 1 — Ownership Governance

Every deliverable must have a clear owner.

Questions:

* Who owns the outcome?
* Who is accountable?
* Who executes?

Governance Rule:

No deliverable should have multiple accountable owners.

---

## Layer 2 — Decision Governance

Every decision requires:

* ownership
* authority
* timeline
* escalation path

Questions:

* Who makes the decision?
* Who approves the decision?
* How quickly should it be made?

Governance Rule:

Every decision must have a decision owner.

---

## Layer 3 — Escalation Governance

Escalation should be structured.

Not emotional.

Questions:

* When should escalation occur?
* Who receives escalations?
* What constitutes escalation?

Governance Rule:

Issues should be resolved at the lowest responsible level.

---

## Layer 4 — Approval Governance

Approvals create friction.

Governance should minimize unnecessary approvals.

Questions:

* Which approvals are required?
* Which approvals create bottlenecks?
* Which approvals can be delegated?

Governance Rule:

Every approval must have a business justification.

---

## Layer 5 — Executive Governance

Executives should govern the system.

Not operate the system.

Questions:

* What decisions require executive involvement?
* What issues should never reach executives?

Governance Rule:

Executives should focus on strategic decisions.

# Governance Maturity Model™

## Level 1 — Hero Driven

Decisions rely on individuals.

Governance is informal.

---

## Level 2 — Process Driven

Processes exist.

Governance is inconsistent.

---

## Level 3 — Governance Driven

Ownership and approvals are documented.

Escalations are structured.

---

## Level 4 — Operational Governance

Governance actively improves delivery performance.

---

## Level 5 — Delivery Intelligence™

Governance continuously adapts using delivery intelligence metrics.
