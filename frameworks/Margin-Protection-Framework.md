# Margin Protection Framework™

Version 1.0

The Margin Protection Framework™ is designed to help ERP Implementation Firms and Systems Integrators identify, measure, and eliminate sources of margin erosion throughout the delivery lifecycle.

Most firms believe margins are lost during sales.

In reality, margins are often lost during delivery.

Margin Protection™ exists to identify the operational, governance, resource, and execution failures that quietly destroy profitability.

---

# The Margin Problem

Common symptoms:

* project overruns
* excessive rework
* scope creep
* resource inefficiencies
* delivery delays
* executive firefighting
* write-offs

The result:

Revenue grows.

Profitability declines.

---

# Objective

Protect profitability throughout the delivery lifecycle.

Create predictable margins.

Create scalable delivery economics.

# The Five Layers of Margin Protection™

## Layer 1 — Scope Protection

Can delivery teams control scope expansion?

Measures:

* scope creep frequency
* change request discipline
* project boundary clarity

---

## Layer 2 — Resource Protection

Can resources be deployed efficiently?

Measures:

* utilization efficiency
* resource allocation effectiveness
* staffing accuracy

---

## Layer 3 — Delivery Protection

Can projects remain on plan?

Measures:

* schedule adherence
* milestone performance
* delivery predictability

---

## Layer 4 — Governance Protection

Can governance prevent profitability erosion?

Measures:

* decision latency
* escalation costs
* approval efficiency

---

## Layer 5 — Financial Protection

Can leadership identify margin risk early?

Measures:

* project profitability visibility
* forecast accuracy
* margin variance
