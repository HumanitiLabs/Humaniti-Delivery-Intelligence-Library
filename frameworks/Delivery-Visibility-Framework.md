# Delivery Visibility Framework™

Version 1.0

The Delivery Visibility Framework™ is designed to help ERP Implementation Firms and Systems Integrators identify delivery risks before they become delivery failures.

---

# Problem

Most delivery organizations operate reactively.

Leadership often discovers:

* project delays
* resource conflicts
* scope creep
* governance breakdowns
* margin erosion

after they have already occurred.

The result is:

* escalations
* firefighting
* executive intervention
* reduced margins
* reduced delivery capacity

---

# Objective

Create visibility before escalation.

Create intelligence before failure.

Create governance before chaos.

---

# The Four Layers of Delivery Visibility™

## Layer 1 — Project Visibility

Can leadership see:

* milestone status
* delivery health
* project risks
* scope changes
* client escalations

Questions:

* Are project dashboards current?
* Are risks documented?
* Are milestones measurable?

---

## Layer 2 — Portfolio Visibility

Can leadership see:

* delivery trends
* portfolio health
* delivery bottlenecks
* project concentration risk

Questions:

* Which projects are at risk?
* Which clients create the most escalations?
* Which engagements generate the highest margins?

---

## Layer 3 — Resource Visibility

Can leadership see:

* utilization
* staffing gaps
* resource conflicts
* key-person dependency

Questions:

* Who is overloaded?
* Who is underutilized?
* Which projects depend on one person?

---

## Layer 4 — Executive Visibility

Can leadership see:

* governance breakdowns
* escalation trends
* decision bottlenecks
* margin leakage

Questions:

* What requires executive attention?
* What should never have reached executive attention?

---

# Visibility Maturity Levels

Level 1 — Reactive

Issues discovered after failure.

Level 2 — Reporting Driven

Issues visible through manual reporting.

Level 3 — Operational Visibility

Issues identified before escalation.

Level 4 — Predictive Visibility

Future risks become visible.

Level 5 — Delivery Intelligence™

Visibility continuously drives operational decisions.
