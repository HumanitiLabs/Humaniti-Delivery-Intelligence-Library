# Delivery Intelligence Library System

Purpose

The Humaniti Delivery Intelligence Library serves as the open-source knowledge base for Delivery Intelligence™.

Every asset inside this repository must strengthen at least one pillar of Delivery Intelligence™.

---

## Pillars

1. Delivery Capacity
2. Delivery Visibility
3. Delivery Governance
4. Margin Protection
5. Resource Intelligence

---

## Asset Types

### Assessments

Measure current state.

### Frameworks

Explain and structure thinking.

### Scorecards

Create measurable benchmarks.

### SOPs

Standardize execution.

### SLAs

Define operational commitments.

### Benchmarks

Compare maturity and performance.

### Templates

Accelerate implementation.

---

## Acceptance Criteria

Every asset must:

* solve a real delivery problem
* improve operational visibility
* improve governance
* improve scalability
* be understandable by delivery leaders
* be usable by ERP implementation firms and systems integrators

---

## Long-Term Mission

Build the world's largest open-source repository of Delivery Intelligence™ frameworks, methodologies, assessments, scorecards, SOPs, governance models, benchmarks, and operating standards.
