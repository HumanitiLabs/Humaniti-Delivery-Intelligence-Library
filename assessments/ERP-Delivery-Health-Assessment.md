# ERP Delivery Health Assessment™

Version 1.0

The ERP Delivery Health Assessment™ is designed to measure the overall health, scalability, visibility, governance, and operational effectiveness of ERP implementation firms and systems integrators.

---

# Scoring Model

The assessment measures five pillars of Delivery Intelligence™.

Each pillar is scored from 0-20.

Total possible score:

100

---

# Pillar 1 — Delivery Capacity

Can the organization increase delivery volume without increasing operational chaos?

Questions:

1. Projects consistently meet committed milestones.
2. Delivery teams have available capacity to absorb additional work.
3. Resource allocation is proactive rather than reactive.
4. Project managers are not overloaded.
5. Leadership is confident in future delivery capacity.

Score:

1-5 for each question.

---

# Pillar 2 — Delivery Visibility

Can leadership identify delivery risks before projects fail?

Questions:

1. Leadership has real-time visibility into project health.
2. Delivery risks are identified early.
3. Escalations rarely come as surprises.
4. Project status reporting is accurate.
5. Delivery performance is measurable.

Score:

1-5 for each question.

---

# Pillar 3 — Delivery Governance

Can decisions move efficiently through the organization?

Questions:

1. Ownership is clearly defined.
2. Escalation paths are documented.
3. Approvals occur within expected timelines.
4. Stakeholders know who makes decisions.
5. Governance reduces delays rather than creating them.

Score:

1-5 for each question.

---

# Pillar 4 — Margin Protection

Can the organization protect profitability during delivery?

Questions:

1. Project profitability is actively monitored.
2. Scope changes are controlled.
3. Rework is minimized.
4. Resource utilization is optimized.
5. Delivery performance supports target margins.

Score:

1-5 for each question.

---

# Pillar 5 — Resource Intelligence

Can leadership make informed staffing decisions?

Questions:

1. Resource utilization is visible.
2. Staffing gaps are identified early.
3. Demand forecasting exists.
4. Key-person dependency is low.
5. Resource allocation supports strategic priorities.

Score:

1-5 for each question.
