# Delivery Visibility Audit™

Version 1.0

The Delivery Visibility Audit™ evaluates how effectively leadership can identify, monitor, and respond to delivery risks before they become delivery failures.

Most organizations discover problems after escalation.

Delivery Visibility™ exists to create visibility before escalation.

The objective is to identify blind spots that reduce delivery performance, create surprises, and increase leadership intervention.

---

# Audit Overview

The audit evaluates five dimensions:

1. Project Visibility
2. Portfolio Visibility
3. Resource Visibility
4. Executive Visibility
5. Risk Visibility

Each dimension is scored from 0–20.

Total Possible Score:

100

---

# Scoring Scale

1 = Strongly Disagree

2 = Disagree

3 = Neutral

4 = Agree

5 = Strongly Agree

---

# Dimension 1 — Project Visibility

Questions:

1. Project health is visible in real time.
2. Milestone status is consistently updated.
3. Project risks are documented.
4. Scope changes are tracked.
5. Escalations rarely come as surprises.

---

# Dimension 2 — Portfolio Visibility

Questions:

1. Leadership can see portfolio-wide delivery health.
2. At-risk projects are easily identified.
3. Delivery trends are monitored.
4. Client concentration risk is visible.
5. Portfolio performance is measurable.

---

# Dimension 3 — Resource Visibility

Questions:

1. Resource utilization is visible.
2. Staffing gaps are identified early.
3. Resource conflicts are visible.
4. Capacity constraints are measurable.
5. Resource forecasts are accurate.

---

# Dimension 4 — Executive Visibility

Questions:

1. Leadership receives actionable delivery intelligence.
2. Executive reporting is reliable.
3. Escalation trends are visible.
4. Governance issues are measurable.
5. Leadership can identify delivery risks quickly.

---

# Dimension 5 — Risk Visibility

Questions:

1. Delivery risks are identified early.
2. Risk ownership is documented.
3. Risk mitigation actions are tracked.
4. Risks rarely become surprises.
5. Risk reporting supports decision making.
