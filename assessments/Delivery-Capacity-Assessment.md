# Delivery Capacity Assessment™

Version 1.0

The Delivery Capacity Assessment™ evaluates an organization's ability to increase delivery volume, absorb growth, maintain profitability, and scale operations without creating operational chaos.

Most ERP Implementation Firms and Systems Integrators believe capacity is a resource problem.

In reality, delivery capacity is influenced by resources, governance, operations, knowledge, and leadership.

This assessment identifies the constraints limiting sustainable delivery growth.

---

# Assessment Overview

The assessment evaluates five dimensions:

1. Resource Capacity
2. Operational Capacity
3. Governance Capacity
4. Knowledge Capacity
5. Leadership Capacity

Each dimension is scored from 0–20.

Total Possible Score:

100

# Dimension 1 — Resource Capacity

Questions:

1. We have sufficient resources to support current delivery demand.
2. Resource shortages are identified before impacting projects.
3. Capacity planning occurs regularly.
4. Staffing decisions are proactive rather than reactive.
5. Leadership understands available delivery capacity.

Score:

Resource Capacity Score = Total ÷ 25 × 20

---

# Dimension 2 — Operational Capacity

Questions:

1. Delivery workflows are standardized.
2. Handoffs between teams are efficient.
3. Delivery processes support scaling.
4. Operational bottlenecks are identified quickly.
5. Increased project volume does not create operational instability.

Score:

Operational Capacity Score = Total ÷ 25 × 20

# Dimension 3 — Governance Capacity

Questions:

1. Decision making supports delivery speed.
2. Approval processes are efficient.
3. Governance does not create unnecessary delays.
4. Escalation paths are clearly defined.
5. Delivery teams are empowered to make decisions.

# Dimension 4 — Knowledge Capacity

Questions:

1. Critical delivery knowledge is documented.
2. New team members become productive quickly.
3. Delivery success does not depend on a small number of individuals.
4. SOPs are available and maintained.
5. Knowledge transfer occurs consistently.

Knowledge Capacity Score = Total ÷ 25 × 20

# Dimension 5 — Leadership Capacity

Questions:

1. Leadership focuses primarily on strategic priorities.
2. Executives are not involved in routine delivery decisions.
3. Delivery teams operate independently.
4. Escalations rarely require executive intervention.
5. Leadership can support growth without becoming a bottleneck.
