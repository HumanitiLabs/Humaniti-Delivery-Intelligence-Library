# Executive Dependency Assessment™

Version 1.0

The Executive Dependency Assessment™ measures the extent to which delivery performance depends on executive involvement, executive decision making, executive escalation handling, and executive intervention.

Organizations cannot scale delivery if leadership becomes the operating system.

The objective of this assessment is to identify where executive involvement is creating operational fragility and limiting delivery scalability.

---

# Assessment Overview

The assessment evaluates five dimensions:

1. Decision Dependency
2. Escalation Dependency
3. Approval Dependency
4. Knowledge Dependency
5. Delivery Dependency

Each dimension is scored from 0–20.

Total Possible Score:

100

---

# Scoring Scale

1 = Strongly Disagree

2 = Disagree

3 = Neutral

4 = Agree

5 = Strongly Agree

---

# Dimension 1 — Decision Dependency

Questions:

1. Most delivery decisions can be made without executive involvement.
2. Teams understand decision authority.
3. Delivery managers are empowered to make decisions.
4. Executives are rarely required for routine approvals.
5. Decision bottlenecks rarely involve leadership.

Score:

Decision Dependency Score = Total ÷ 25 × 20

---

# Dimension 2 — Escalation Dependency

Questions:

1. Most delivery issues are resolved below the executive level.
2. Escalations rarely reach executives.
3. Escalation ownership is clearly defined.
4. Delivery teams resolve issues independently.
5. Executive intervention is exceptional rather than routine.

Score:

Escalation Dependency Score = Total ÷ 25 × 20

---

# Dimension 3 — Approval Dependency

Questions:

1. Approval authority is delegated appropriately.
2. Executives do not approve routine delivery activities.
3. Approval bottlenecks rarely involve leadership.
4. Teams can progress work without executive sign-off.
5. Approval governance supports delivery speed.

Score:

Approval Dependency Score = Total ÷ 25 × 20

---

# Dimension 4 — Knowledge Dependency

Questions:

1. Critical delivery knowledge is documented.
2. Processes are not dependent on executive memory.
3. Teams can operate without executive guidance.
4. Knowledge transfer is effective.
5. Delivery success does not rely on a small number of leaders.

Score:

Knowledge Dependency Score = Total ÷ 25 × 20

---

# Dimension 5 — Delivery Dependency

Questions:

1. Projects can operate successfully without executive involvement.
2. Delivery teams are self-sufficient.
3. Leadership focuses on strategy rather than operations.
4. Delivery performance remains stable during executive absence.
5. Delivery accountability exists throughout the organization.

Score:

Delivery Dependency Score = Total ÷ 25 × 20

# Executive Dependency Levels

## Level 1 — Independent Delivery Organization

81–100

Leadership governs.

The organization operates.

Executive dependency is minimal.

---

## Level 2 — Healthy Delegation

61–80

Minor leadership involvement required.

Most delivery activity remains distributed.

---

## Level 3 — Growing Executive Dependency

41–60

Leadership increasingly involved in operational decisions.

Scalability becoming constrained.

---

## Level 4 — Executive Bottleneck

21–40

Leadership is actively involved in delivery operations.

Decision making slows.

Delivery capacity suffers.

---

## Level 5 — Founder / Executive Operating System

0–20

The organization depends heavily on executives for delivery success.

Operational fragility is high.

Scalability is severely constrained.
