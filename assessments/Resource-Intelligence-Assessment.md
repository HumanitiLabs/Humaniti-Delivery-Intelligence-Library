# Resource Intelligence Assessment™

Version 1.0

The Resource Intelligence Assessment™ evaluates how effectively an ERP Implementation Firm or Systems Integrator manages delivery capacity, resource allocation, utilization, staffing, forecasting, and dependency risk.

The objective is to identify hidden resource constraints before they impact delivery performance, profitability, client satisfaction, or organizational scalability.

---

# Assessment Overview

The assessment evaluates five dimensions:

1. Capacity Intelligence
2. Utilization Intelligence
3. Demand Intelligence
4. Dependency Intelligence
5. Strategic Resource Intelligence

Each dimension is scored from 0–20.

Total Possible Score:

100

---

# Scoring Scale

1 = Strongly Disagree

2 = Disagree

3 = Neutral

4 = Agree

5 = Strongly Agree

---

# Dimension 1 — Capacity Intelligence

Questions:

1. Leadership has visibility into available delivery capacity.
2. Resource shortages are identified before they impact projects.
3. Capacity planning occurs regularly.
4. Teams can accurately forecast delivery capacity.
5. Delivery growth does not create resource chaos.

Score:

Capacity Intelligence Score = Total ÷ 25 × 20

---

# Dimension 2 — Utilization Intelligence

Questions:

1. Resource utilization is measured consistently.
2. Over-utilization is identified quickly.
3. Under-utilization is actively managed.
4. Utilization targets support sustainable delivery.
5. Resource planning balances utilization and delivery quality.

Score:

Utilization Intelligence Score = Total ÷ 25 × 20

---

# Dimension 3 — Demand Intelligence

Questions:

1. Future delivery demand is forecasted.
2. Resource requirements are understood before projects begin.
3. Hiring decisions are based on projected demand.
4. Pipeline visibility supports staffing decisions.
5. Leadership can anticipate future capacity constraints.

Score:

Demand Intelligence Score = Total ÷ 25 × 20

---

# Dimension 4 — Dependency Intelligence

Questions:

1. Key-person dependency is measured.
2. Critical knowledge is documented.
3. Projects can continue when key individuals are unavailable.
4. Specialized skills are distributed appropriately.
5. Resource concentration risks are actively managed.

Score:

Dependency Intelligence Score = Total ÷ 25 × 20

---

# Dimension 5 — Strategic Resource Intelligence

Questions:

1. High-value projects receive priority staffing.
2. Resource allocation supports strategic objectives.
3. Resource decisions consider profitability.
4. Leadership understands resource ROI.
5. Staffing decisions align with growth plans.

Score:

Strategic Resource Intelligence Score = Total ÷ 25 × 20
