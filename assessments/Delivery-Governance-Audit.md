# Delivery Governance Audit™

Version 1.0

The Delivery Governance Audit™ evaluates the effectiveness of governance across ERP Implementation Firms and Systems Integrators.

The objective is to identify governance bottlenecks, decision friction, escalation dependency, ownership gaps, and executive overload before they impact delivery performance.

---

# Audit Overview

This audit measures five governance dimensions:

1. Ownership Governance
2. Decision Governance
3. Escalation Governance
4. Approval Governance
5. Executive Governance

Each dimension is scored from 0–20.

Total Possible Score:

100

---

# Scoring Scale

1 = Strongly Disagree

2 = Disagree

3 = Neutral

4 = Agree

5 = Strongly Agree

---

# Dimension 1 — Ownership Governance

Questions:

1. Every project deliverable has a clearly defined owner.
2. Team members understand their responsibilities.
3. Accountability is documented and visible.
4. Ownership disputes are rare.
5. Delivery outcomes can be traced to accountable individuals.

Score:

Ownership Governance Score = Total ÷ 25 × 20

---

# Dimension 2 — Decision Governance

Questions:

1. Decision authority is clearly defined.
2. Teams know who can approve decisions.
3. Decisions are made within expected timelines.
4. Decision ownership is documented.
5. Decision delays rarely impact delivery.

Score:

Decision Governance Score = Total ÷ 25 × 20

---

# Dimension 3 — Escalation Governance

Questions:

1. Escalation paths are documented.
2. Escalations occur only when necessary.
3. Teams understand escalation criteria.
4. Escalations are resolved efficiently.
5. Most issues are resolved at the lowest responsible level.

Score:

Escalation Governance Score = Total ÷ 25 × 20

---

# Dimension 4 — Approval Governance

Questions:

1. Approval processes are clearly defined.
2. Approval timelines are predictable.
3. Approval bottlenecks are rare.
4. Approval authority is documented.
5. Unnecessary approvals have been eliminated.

Score:

Approval Governance Score = Total ÷ 25 × 20

---

# Dimension 5 — Executive Governance

Questions:

1. Executives focus primarily on strategic decisions.
2. Executives are not required for routine delivery decisions.
3. Leadership is not overloaded with escalations.
4. Governance enables delegation.
5. Operational decisions remain within delivery teams.

Score:

Executive Governance Score = Total ÷ 25 × 20
